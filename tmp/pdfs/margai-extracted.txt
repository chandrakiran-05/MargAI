1. THE CORE AIM OF MARGAI
Our Core Aim: Eliminate preventable road fatalities, blind-spot crashes, and vehicle
suspension damage across India by connecting forward-facing vehicle cameras into a real-
time, road-locked intelligence network—warning trailing drivers before line-of-sight
while automatically reporting verified road damage to municipal authorities.
Every year, Indian roads witness over 1.77 lakh fatalities and thousands of crores in commercial
fleet repair losses caused by unmarked potholes, sudden pedestrian crossings behind parked vehicles,
and highway pile-ups. Existing civic portals fail because they expect citizens to stop their vehicles and
manually upload photos, while generic map apps broadcast noisy, stale alerts across parallel streets.
MargAI (Marg = Path/Road across pan-Indian languages) solves both problems simultaneously
through four core technical capabilities:
Real-Time Edge Road Observation: Forward-facing cameras run quantized edge AI models
locally on the device to spot potholes, unmarked speed breakers, waterlogging,
pedestrians, stray cattle, and stalled vehicles in under 100 milliseconds.
Dynamic Road-Locked Channel Switching: Using road-snapping geometry, each vehicle
joins a dedicated live channel tied strictly to its exact road segment ID and travel
direction. Turning onto a new street immediately drops the previous road's alerts and streams
hazards exclusively from the new road.
Latest-Only State & Pass-By Auto-Purge: Duplicate sightings of the same pothole merge into
a single updated record so only the freshest condition is stored. On-device forward-cone math
deletes any hazard from the driver's queue the exact second their vehicle drives past
it.
Zero-Screen Vernacular Voice Co-Pilot: Powered by Gemini Live, drivers receive calm,
hands-free audio warnings in Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, or English over car Bluetooth so their
eyes never leave the road.

potholes the instant the first car hits them.

Immediate B2B Cash Flow (No Waiting on Govt Tenders): Selling directly to logistics

Proprietary "Self-Healing" Ground-Truth Data Moat: Every connected vehicle

India's freshest road-health ledger that is highly valuable to commercial auto insurers,
Smart City authorities, and highway contractors.
Lean Cloud & Edge Economics: Running vision locally on the device and syncing only
lightweight coordinate packets keeps bandwidth under 5 MB per driving hour—enabling
massive national scale at minimal server cost.
